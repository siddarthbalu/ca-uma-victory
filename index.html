CA Uma - Victory

CA Final Group II revision planner — 36 days, 3 full revisions, built around the Nov 2026 papers.

Download for Android
Version 1.0 · 59 KB · Android 8.0 or newer · Free · Works offline

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway — it is only a planner.
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification, or Files › Downloads).
  3. If your phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome (or WhatsApp / Files), then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details › Install anyway. It shows this for any app that isn't from the Play Store.
  5. Open CA Uma - Victory, pick a plan layout and your start date. Done.

What's inside

  • Today — your sessions for the day; mark Done / Partly / Skipped and log hours.
  • Plan — all 36 days. Change any session, swap or add days to suit you.
  • Chapters — tick every DT, IDT and IBS chapter per revision and rate your confidence.
  • Mistake Log — what went wrong in mocks and practice, and the correct rule.
  • Progress — hours, sessions, mocks and chapters at a glance.
  • Two layouts: 3 sessions a day, or DT + IDT every day. Countdown to each paper.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone — no sign-up, no internet needed. Use Settings › Back up my data to keep a copy in Google Drive, and Share my plan with a friend to send your plan (without your progress). Installing a newer version over this one keeps your data.

Exam dates are from ICAI's Nov 2026 timetable as reported — always verify on your admit card.
